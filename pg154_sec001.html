Agriculture for Secondary Schools

Glossary

Artificial insemination (AI)

a breeding technique where semen is

collected from a male animal and manually

inserted into the female’s reproductive tract.

Bacon

a type of meat product that comes from

the back or sides of a pig, often cured and

smoked.

Bagging

the practice of covering a bunch of bananas

with bags to protect the fruits against damage

caused by insects and other pests.

Banana bunch

large cluster of bananas growing on a single

stalk.

Banana finger

an individual banana within a hand.

Banana hand

a subgroup of bananas on a bunch. Each hand

consists of 10-20 bananas.

Barrow

a castrated male pig, usually raised for meat

production.

Biosecurity

biological practices designed to prevent the

introduction and spread of diseases within an

animal unit.

Boar

a mature male pig used for breeding. Boars

are selected for their genetics to improve the

quality of the pig herd.

Buck

a male rabbit.

Castration

the removal of the testicles of a young

male animal to prevent it from becoming a

breeding animal (boar).

Circadian rhythms

the natural physical mental and behaviour changes

that occur in living organism including sows

that follows a 24 hours cycle of which light

and dark have biggest influence.
