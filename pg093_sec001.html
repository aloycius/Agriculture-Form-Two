(d) Marketing

Effectively

marketing

potatoes

requires a combination of strategies.

Here are some of the strategies:

(i) Understand the potato market:

Conduct surveys to understand

consumer preferences, buying

habits, and trends.

(ii) Product differentiation: Promote

different varieties of round

potatoes and their specific uses.

It is important to offer convenient

packaging sizes for various

consumer needs.

(iii) Branding and messaging: Convince the market by creating a story

about the potatoes and highlighting, e.g., health benefits and organic

certification. Showcase how potatoes can be prepared (baked, mashed,

fried, or roasted).

(iv) Marketing channels: Use platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and

WhatsApp to reach a large audience.

(v) Partnerships and collaborations: You can collaborate with restaurants

and catering service providers to feature potatoes in their dishes.

(vi) Promotions and events: Offer discounts and participate in farmers’

markets to engage directly with consumers.

(vii)Advertising: Create and use online food blogs and social media accounts.

Also, posters can be fixed strategically to reach a broader audience.
