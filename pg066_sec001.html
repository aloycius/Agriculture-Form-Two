Sweet potato and its importance

Sweet potato (Ipomoea batatas) is a root crop primarily cultivated for its starchy

tubers, though its leaves are also edible. It is rich in vitamins, minerals, and

beta-carotene. It can be orange, white, or purple (Figure 4.1(b). It is produced

throughout Tanzania for market in both rural and urban areas. The major producing

regions in Tanzania include Shinyanga, Mara, Mwanza, Kagera, Kigoma, Tabora,

Morogoro, and Mbeya. Both local and improved varieties are grown in Tanzania.

Improved varieties include Kabode, Kakamega, Mataya, Kiegea, Ejumla/Mlezi,

Polista, and Ukerewe.
