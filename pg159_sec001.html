Agriculture for Secondary Schools

Nest box

a box provided to the doe where she can give

birth and care for her kits.

Offspring

the young of an animal.

Parasites

organisms like mites, fleas, or worms that can

infest animals like rabbits and pigs, causing

health problems.

Parturition

the act of giving birth. It has the same

meaning as farrowing.

Pedigree

a recorded lineage or ancestry of an animal,

often used for breeding purposes.

Pellet feed

type of concentrated feed made from ground-

up grains and other ingredients, formulated

to meet the nutritional needs of animals e.g.,

rabbits.

Piglet

young pig, usually under the age of 3-4

weeks, before it is weaned from its mother.

Plantain

the type of banana that is starchy rather than

sweet and is often used in cooking

Porcine

a term relating to pigs or swine. It is often

used in veterinary medicine and scientific

research.

Prophylactic

a treatment designed to prevent disease

occurrence.

Pseudostem

the false stem or trunk of the banana plant.

Quarantine

the isolation of new or sick animals to prevent

the spread of disease to the rest of the herd.

Rabbitry

a facility or farm where rabbits are bred and

raised.

Run

an outdoor space where rabbits can exercise

and graze.

Setts

sections of cane stalks with buds used for

propagation in sugarcane.

Sexing

the process of determining the sex of a rabbit,

usually performed on young rabbits.
