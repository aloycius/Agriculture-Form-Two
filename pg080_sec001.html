Choosing a site for round potato production

The crop grows in well-drained, loamy soils rich in organic matter and requires

an annual rainfall of 800 -1750 mm. In Tanzania, round potatoes grow well at

altitudes ranging from 1200 to 2700 m above sea level. The optimal temperature

for growing round potatoes is 15 - 20 ºC and soil pH ranges from 5.5 to 7.0.

In soils with a pH value less than 5.0, limestone should be applied at 1 ton per

hectare to improve round potato production.

Land preparation

The field for round potatoes should be cleaned well. After clearing, the next step

is tilling; this helps to mix the soil, reduce weeds, improve water infiltration and

soil aeration, and mix the remains of the previous season’s crops with the soil.

Till the soil to a 30-45 cm depth to create a fine seedbed. Avoid burning residues

in the field to protect the environment and the organisms contributing to soil

fertility. Round potatoes have a higher demand for nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P),

and potassium (K) than for other plant nutrients. Improve the soil structure by

adding organic matter or compost. In areas where manure is available, 25 tons

of well-rotted manure per hectare will improve the production of round potatoes

and other crops that will follow in the cropping cycle. It is essential to apply

fertilisers based on the soil test results of a given field.
