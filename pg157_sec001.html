Agriculture for Secondary Schools

Forage

plant materials, such as hay, grass, or leafy

greens, that animals like rabbits eat.

Fostering

the practice of transferring piglets from

one sow to another to ensure that all piglets

receive adequate milk and care. This is often

done when a sow has too many piglets to

nurse.

Fur block

a condition where rabbits ingest too much

fur, leading to a blockage in their digestive

system.

Fur breeds

Breeds that are primarily raised for their fur,

such as the Angora rabbits

Gestation

the period between conception and birth in

animals; in pigs, typically lasting about 114

days (3 months, 3 weeks, 3 days). It is around

28-31 days for rabbits

Gilt

a young female pig that has not yet given

birth. Gilts are typically bred when they

reach maturity.

Grooming

the process of cleaning and maintaining a

rabbit’s fur, which can include brushing,

trimming, and bathing.

Harvesting

the process of collecting mature crops from

the field.

Herbivore

an animal that primarily eats plants, as rabbits

do.

Herd

a group of animals e.g. pigs raised together.

Hutch

a type of cage or enclosure where rabbits are

housed.

Inbreeding

breeding between closely related animals

e.g. rabbits or pigs, often to maintain specific

traits within a line, but it can also lead to

health issues.
