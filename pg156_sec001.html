Cuttings

the sections of stems that are planted to

propagate new plants.

Dam

adult female animal e.g. rabbit for breeding

purpose

Dessert banana

bananas that are sweet in flavour eaten fresh

and raw, after the main part of the meal.

Doe

a female rabbit.

Estrus (Heat)

the period when a female animal e.g. a pig is

fertile and ready to be bred. Sows and gilts

usually come into heat every 21 days.

Farrowing

the process of giving birth in pigs. This is a

critical time for both the sow and her piglets,

requiring careful management.

Farrowing index

a measure of the reproductive performance

of a sow, often calculated as the number of

litters produced per year.

Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

a measure of an animal’s efficiency in

converting feed into body mass., This is

important in evaluating the productivity of

an animal.

Feeder pig

a young pig that has been weaned and is

being fed to gain weight before it is sold for

finishing or slaughter.

Finishing

the stage in pig production where pigs are fed

to reach market weight, typically around 90-

100 kg. This is the last phase before slaughter.

Flushing

the practice of increasing the feed provided

to female animals e.g. sows and gilts before

breeding to improve their body condition and

increase the number of eggs released during

ovulation.
