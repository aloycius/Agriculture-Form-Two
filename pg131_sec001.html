Breeding practices

Good breeding practices help produce healthy piglets and ensure the next

generation is strong. A healthy pig with good traits (like fast growth or strong

immunity) should be chosen for breeding. The pregnant sows (mother pigs)

should be cared for, by providing proper food and a comfortable place to give

birth. Effective management of the breeding herd, which includes breeding

boars and gilts, is crucial for ensuring the productivity and sustainability of pig

production on the farm.
