Agriculture for Secondary Schools

Preface

This textbook, Agriculture for Secondary Schools, is explicitly written for Form

Two students in the United Republic of Tanzania. It is prepared according to

the 2023 Agriculture Syllabus for Ordinary Secondary Education Form I - IV

issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. Some of the

contents in this book have been adapted from the Agriculture for Secondary

Schools Student’s Book Form One (2021), Form Two (2021), and Form Three

(2022) which were published in accordance with the 2019 agriculture syllabus

for Ordinary Level Secondary Education, issued by the Ministry of Education

Science and Technology.

The book consists of eight chapters namely, General principles and practices

of the production of crops, Banana production, Cassava production, Sweet

potato production, and Round potato production. Other chapters are General

principles and practices of livestock production, Pig production, and Rabbit

production. Each chapter contains texts, illustrations, activities, and exercises.

You are encouraged to do all the activities and attempt all the exercises and other

assignments provided by your teacher. Doing so will enable you to develop the

intended competencies.
