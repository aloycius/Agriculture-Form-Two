Soil water management

Round potatoes need consistent moisture, especially during tuber formation.

Avoid waterlogging, which can lead to diseases. The use of drip, sprinkler, or

furrow irrigation to supply moisture in the field can enhance water use efficiency.

Low and variable soil moisture levels lead to reduced dry matter and poor tuber

set. Planting potatoes on mounds also enhances water-soil conservation.
