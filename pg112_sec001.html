Agriculture for Secondary Schools

Activity 6.7

1. Through the library and internet search, find out the meaning, uses, and

characteristics of the following, in livestock production:

(a) Slaughter slab;

(b) Slaughterhouse; and

(c) Abattoir.

2. Visit one of the following nearby structures in your community: slaughter

slab/house/ abattoir and study on:

(a) Characteristics features of the structures

(b) Slaughtering procedures for the following animals:

(i) Ruminant animals;

(ii) Chicken; and

(iii) Rabbit.

Exercise 6.3

Use various learning resources, including library, internet searches, and resource

persons, to answer the following questions:

1. Giving four examples in each case, differentiate livestock products from

livestock by-products.

2. With examples, outline five characteristics of good quality packaging

materials used for animal products.

3. Outline any four importance of processing livestock products.

4. Explain four various ways of enhancing the shelf-life of livestock products.

5. Identify five components of a slaughter (slab) house.

6. Describe the basic characteristics of a good vehicle for transporting livestock

animals.
