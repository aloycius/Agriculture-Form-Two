Livestock farm record keeping

Record keeping is necessary for the effectiveness and efficiency performance of

any livestock production enterprise. A livestock register is a tool used to record

and keep relevant information on animal farms. Some information includes the

number of animals on the farm, animal identification number, date of birth, sire

number, dam number, calf and its sex, date of calving, date of purchase, and date

of sale/auction/death. Financial records (including costs and revenue) and health

records are among the important information to be included in a farm register.
