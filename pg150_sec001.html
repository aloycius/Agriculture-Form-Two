Marketing of rabbit

Rabbit farming can be done for different market needs such as meat production,

production of breeding stock, educational and medical laboratory purposes, for

pets, show animals, and for wool and skin. The market for the rabbits can be

selling live rabbits to commercial processors or laboratory animal suppliers.

Alternatively, you may dress and sell the rabbit meat to friends, relatives,

neighbours, restaurants, or stores. Each of these markets needs clean, healthy,

and well-prepared meat.
